# Assay — single-take run sheet

**Audio and picture recorded together, one pass, 5:00.**

Read this on a second screen or on paper. `SAY` is verbatim. `DO` is the action
and *when* it happens relative to the words. `— pause —` is a real silence: stop
talking, let the frame settle, count it.

---

## The structure, and why

**The landing page explains. The console proves.**

The landing sequence walks the settlement apart one line at a time, with the
object carved away beside each figure, and it has two beats the console cannot
match: a dedicated **breakpoint** frame at ₹11,44,000 — *"this is where you
stopped counting"* — and a **gap** frame that stacks the five lines below it. That
is the argument, told better than a table can tell it.

So the landing carries the explanation, and the console then proves it is a
running system rather than a story: live figures, the drawer showing a policy
line with no rate in it, a countdown off the server's clock, and the letter.

Roughly **3 minutes landing, 1 minute console, 1 minute limits and close.**

## Two different scrolling rules

| Where | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Landing** | **Speak, then scroll.** Each step activates on scroll position. Say the line, then push to bring the next one up. |
| **Console** | **Move, stop, speak.** Never scroll while saying a number — the figure must be still while the viewer hears it. |

## Sixty seconds before you hit record

1. Warm the deploy — load both, once:
   <https://assay-web.onrender.com/> and
   <https://assay-web.onrender.com/s/stl_2608mera01>
2. Back to the landing page. `⌘⇧R`. Scroll to the very top. Wait for the object
   to settle.
3. Open the console in a **second tab** and confirm the strip reads **closing**,
   the countdown is moving, the demo chip reads `state · live`. Then switch back
   to tab 1. Segment 6 becomes `⌘⌥→` instead of a click and a load.
4. Focus on. Dock hidden. Menu bar hidden. Slack quit. Phone face down.
5. Zoom 110–125%. No bookmarks bar.
6. `⌘⇧5` → record **the window**, mic **on**, **Show Mouse Clicks off**.
7. Three seconds of silence before the first word. You will want the handle.

## The seven cut points

At each `▣` the screen is static and you are silent, so a hard cut is invisible.
Run it continuously — but if you fluff a line, **stop, go back to the last cut
point, and start from there.** A mistake costs one segment, not five minutes.

The intellectual core. If a panel remembers one minute, it is this one.

**DO:** scroll until `100% reconciled` fills the frame. Stop.

> **SAY:** So it reconciles. All of it. Every rupee of the seventy-one thousand
> and eighty-two is accounted for.

— pause —

> **SAY:** Now the harder question. How much of it can she *check*?

**DO:** scroll to the ceiling beat.

— pause, 2 seconds —

> **SAY:** Sixty-six point two four percent. Forty-seven thousand and eighty-two
> she can verify against fields she was actually handed.
>
> The other third — twenty-four thousand, the gateway fee itself — she cannot.

**DO:** scroll one step.

> **SAY:** That fee ran over five rails. Three of them reach her report labelled

> **SAY:** Three named fields close the gap. Instrument sub-type, eighteen

> **SAY:** This is what she sends. Every line she disputes, the citation behind

# SEGMENT 7 · honest limits · 4:05–4:35

Do not soften this and do not speed up. This segment is worth more than the demo.

**SCREEN:** the console, still. No cursor movement.

> **SAY:** What it does not do.

— pause —

> **SAY:** The cycle is synthetic and seeded — reproducible from one number. The
> live Razorpay adapter is written and committed, but it has never run against
> real keys.

— pause —

> **SAY:** On the live path, failed attempts stay at zero, because no recon
> report can ever contain one. I would rather return a zero than a plausible
> number.

— pause —

> **SAY:** Recovering the instrument sub-type means nine hundred and sixty extra
> API calls, one per settled row. I have not paid that cost. Where the rail
> cannot answer, the cycle carries a named gap — the field, where we looked, and
> how many rows it hits — instead of a guess.

— pause, 2 seconds —

**▣ CUT POINT 7**

# IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG MID-TAKE

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| You fluff a word | Stop. Back to the last cut point. Re-run from there. |
| A landing step does not activate | You scrolled past it. Scroll back up one push and wait — it activates on position, not on time. |
| The scroll overshoots two steps | Keep going. Do not scroll backwards on camera. Say the line you landed on and drop the one you skipped. |
| The drawer will not close | Click the dark area outside it. Escape also works. |
| First load is slow | You skipped the warm-up. Stop, load both pages, start again. |
| The strip already says `expired` | The demo chip is stuck. Click it, choose `live`, reload. |
| A figure disagrees with this script | **Stop recording.** Fix the script, not the screen. The screen is the evidence. |
